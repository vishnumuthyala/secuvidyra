Privacy information

Current website version: 13 November 2026.

Enquiry preparation

This static site does not send form entries to a server or save them in browser storage. With no admissions email configured, the form creates a text file on your device. With an admissions email configured, it opens your email application with a draft for you to review and send.

What you choose to provide

The enquiry asks for your name, email address, course interest and optional background/timing information. Do not include passwords, identity documents or payment information. You can clear the form or close the page without sending it.

Email and WhatsApp

If you choose an email or WhatsApp contact, those services process your communication under their own policies. Downloading an enquiry does not notify the academy. Once admissions opens, the operator must publish contact and retention information before collecting learner records.

Hosting and cookies

This release includes no analytics, advertising trackers, cookies or external fonts. The hosting provider may process ordinary request information, such as IP addresses and access logs, to serve and secure the website.

Enrolment data

No payments, student accounts or enrolment records are collected by this release. Separate privacy information and operator contact details must accompany any future registration service.
